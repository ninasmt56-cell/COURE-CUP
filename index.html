3 Modular Cup Variants

S-CUP 💖 Sodium-conscious flavour architecture

S-CUP (Pink Variant)

Flavor architecture using oyster mushroom & alliums to reduce added salt requirement.

MODULE COMPOSITION (20g): Oyster Mushroom 5g, Garlic 2g, Shallot 2g, Sorghum 5g, MOCAF 4g, Basil Seed 2g.
M-CUP 💚 Resistant-starch and protein emphasis

M-CUP (Green Variant)

Focuses on resistant starch fractions and plant protein for sustained metabolic energy.

MODULE COMPOSITION (20g): Unripe Banana Flour 7g, MOCAF 4g, Tempeh Flour 4g, Sorghum 3g, Mung Bean 2g.
L-CUP 💜 Viscous soluble fibre & plant-bioactive

L-CUP (Purple Variant)

Rich in viscous soluble fibre (Porang Glucomannan & Basil Seed) for lipid priority.

MODULE COMPOSITION (20g): Porang Glucomannan 6g, Basil Seed 4g, Rice Bran 3g, Tempeh Flour 2g, Sorghum 3g, Unripe Banana Flour 2g.
